CNC Thread Program Generator - Every Thread Type, Free, No Software Install

Threading is one of the parts of CNC turning where a small mistake in the G-code costs a scrapped part. CNCVEYRA's Thread Program Generator builds ready-to-run G-code for full and half threads, internal or external, single or multi-start - directly in your browser.

Open the Thread Generator →

What it generates

  • Full and half thread cycles
  • Internal and external threading
  • Single-start and multi-start threads
  • Metric and inch pitch input

Why use it instead of writing threads by hand

Manual threading programs are written by feel, and calculating passes, depth per pass, and lead-in/lead-out by hand is where most threading errors come from - especially on half threads and multi-start threads. This tool does the calculation for you and outputs a program you can check before it ever touches the machine.

No CAM software required

You don't need CAM software, a license, or a post-processor to get a working threading program - this runs entirely in the browser, for free.

Built by a working CNC programmer

The logic behind this generator was written from scratch by a CNC programmer/machinist with 10+ years of hands-on manual G-code and CAM experience, and the generated programs have been tested on real production machines - not just simulated.
