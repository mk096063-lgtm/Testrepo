Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

CNCVEYRA ("we", "us", "our") operates cncveyra.in, including the CNC Thread Program Generator, CNC Code Lab, CNC Live Drawing tool, and the CNC Guide articles. This page explains what data we collect, how it is used, and the choices you have.

1. Information We Collect

Most tools on this site (Thread Program Generator, Code Lab, Live Drawing) run directly in your browser. The dimensions, feeds, speeds, and drawings you enter into these tools are processed to generate G-code and are not stored on our servers unless you explicitly submit them through a contact or quote form.

When you use the "Get a Quote" or career/application forms, the details you enter (name, company, WhatsApp number, email, project requirement, etc.) are sent directly to us via WhatsApp. We do not store this information in a database on our servers.

2. Cookies and Similar Technologies

We may use cookies or browser local storage to remember your preferences (such as language selection or FAQ category) so the site works smoothly across visits. These do not identify you personally.

3. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services, which may collect data according to their own privacy policies:

  • Google AdSense - may show ads on this site and use cookies to serve relevant ads based on your visits to this and other sites. You can manage ad personalization at Google Ads Settings.
  • Google Analytics (if enabled) - helps us understand aggregate site usage (pages visited, time on site) to improve the tools we offer.
  • WhatsApp - used to receive quote requests and job applications you choose to send us.

These third parties may use cookies, web beacons, and similar technologies to collect information for their own purposes. We do not control and are not responsible for their data practices; please review their respective privacy policies.

4. How We Use Information

  • To respond to quote requests, job applications, and general enquiries sent to us.
  • To operate, maintain, and improve the free CNC tools and guides on this site.
  • To display relevant advertising through Google AdSense, where applicable.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We only share information with service providers (such as WhatsApp, for messages you choose to send) needed to operate the site, or where required by law.

6. Children's Privacy

This site is intended for CNC programmers, machinists, and engineering professionals. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Your Choices

  • You can disable cookies in your browser settings at any time.
  • You can opt out of personalized Google ads via Google Ads Settings.
  • You can choose not to submit the quote/application forms; the calculation tools work without any personal data.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision.

9. Contact

For questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, contact us via WhatsApp through the quote form on our homepage.
